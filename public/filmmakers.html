Live · free Pure Alacrity

One production. One record. Free forever.

Script, story graph, breakdown, stripboard, schedule, budget, call sheets and on-set logs, all reading the same production, so the numbers agree everywhere. No account needed, and it works with no internet. It has run real productions since March 2026.

For interactive work, the branch map is the stripboard: every choice the audience can make is costed and scheduled like any other scene.

The rule: nothing a filmmaker already uses for free will ever move behind a paywall. Paid storage, services and live support come later, for productions that grow.
