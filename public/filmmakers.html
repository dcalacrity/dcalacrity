Live · free Pure Alacrity

Every film is a storm. Pure is the eye.

Script, departments, schedule and spend in one clear place, so the bigger the production gets, the calmer it feels. Nothing a filmmaker uses for free will ever move behind a paywall.

Smart, quietly

Pure reads your production and points at what matters: the one thing that needs you, readiness checks with owners, project health, schedule moves that save a day, and what a changed edit affects.

Set up around you

Tell it what you make and what you do. With 54 film jobs to choose from, it shows your tools in the order you’ll use them, with film words explained if you’re new.

The whole production

155 tools across eight stages, from story to plan, set, post, delivery and business, all reading the same production.

Your work is yours

No account needed. It stays on your device and works offline. You decide what leaves it, and you own everything you make.
