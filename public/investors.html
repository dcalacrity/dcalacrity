Why now

Three things broke at once.

D.C Alacrity sits exactly where these three breaks meet, and was born on a film set to fix them.

Trust broke

Deepfake scams are up 40% a year (Entrust 2026 Identity Fraud Report, via NAR). Realtors have a word, “housefishing,” for listings that look better online than in person, and California now requires disclosure when AI alters property images.

Capture got cheap

Headsets scan rooms for free and labs raise billions to generate worlds. Capture is becoming a commodity. What’s scarce is real people, story, verification, and a system that produces it at scale.

Film work became feast or famine

North Carolina lost an estimated fifth of its crew base after its 2014 incentive change (IATSE Local 491). The people who know best how to capture reality need steady, weekday work.

Not three businesses: one pipeline. The Experience Machine is what customers buy. Pure Alacrity is how we make it anywhere. Our films are where both get proven first. Each layer funds, scales or proves the others.

Why we win

Others own one piece. AI labs generate places that don’t exist; headset scans have no people in them; scan firms sell static models; immersive agencies rebuild every job. We own the shoot, the player and the data, so every job runs on our engine and leaves recurring software behind.

Their progress is our supply chain

Better world models, faster restyling and free capture are inputs we can buy. Every improvement the big labs ship makes an Experience Machine cheaper for us to build.

Impact · Wilmington & North Carolina

Film as a 9-to-5. Steady, weekday, local work for the crews who know how to capture reality.

Wilmington built a world-class crew base over 40 years, and it rises and falls with whichever production is in town. Experience Machine work happens every weekday: one-day captures scheduled in Pure, home for dinner. Students graduate with verified credits and a reason to stay.
