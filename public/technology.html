The platform · 1 · Make · Live and free

Pure Alacrity

The operating system for every production, and the machine that builds the Experience Machine.

An independent production runs on six subscriptions and a folder of spreadsheets that disagree with each other. Pure Alacrity reads one production instead: the script, the breakdown, the schedule, the budget, the day on set, post and delivery, with the same numbers in all of them.

Sidequest began in Raleigh in November–December 2024 and continued through 2025, before Pure existed. Development began in January 2026, after a majority of Episodes 1–3 had been shot; Wilmington pickups continued during 2026. The production and an internship at an established Wilmington production house helped define the workflow problems. Pure has run productions since March 2026. Free, no account needed, works with no internet. The Sidequest chronology.

How it scales · in development

One crew here. Certified crews everywhere.

Productions are pop-up companies that assemble for three weeks and then scatter. Pure already holds the script, stripboard, budget and call sheets. Channels make it the place the team actually talks, and that’s how a few producers in Wilmington can direct crews anywhere.

Production channels Beta Q4 2026 · free

#general, #client and one channel per department, created automatically, with crew added from the call sheet. Move a scene and Pure posts it to the channels it affects.

Alacrity Certified crews 2027

Local camera operators and Part 107 pilots learn our capture spec, pass a paid test shoot and get rated after every job. Raleigh and Charlotte first.

Pure Art Department Prototype 2027

The Experience Machine pointed back at film: scan the set, dress it before anyone builds, and send every decision into the budget.

Privacy rule: chat content is never used for analytics, benchmarks or underwriting, and never sent to model providers without the production’s permission. Pure’s core (scheduling, call sheets, channels and on-set logs) stays free forever.

3 · Deliver · Shipped on Quest

Alacrity Player

Authoring a branching story and running one are normally two worlds joined by hand. When Netflix made Bandersnatch, the tooling ended at a flowchart exported to a spreadsheet that engineers implemented by hand.

Here the graph written in Pure Alacrity is exported as one bundle and the Player reads that file directly. The schema is pinned on both sides and a self-test walks a bundle out and back. It is the runtime inside Right Here Right Now! and what Prize Pool VR is being built against. It plays on Meta Quest today as a native app. One codebase is being extended to WebXR links (no install) and iOS and Android gyro apps, where you look around by moving the phone. Those are in development, so soon no experience will need a headset.

2 · Connect · In development

Alacrity Bridge

Bridge is being developed to carry production context through post: the planned scene, recorded take, source media, edit version and delivery remain connected while collaborators work in their own tools. OpenTimelineIO export and the data contract provide an initial handoff; the editor-side panel is still being built. Wider sound, color, VFX, review and release workflows are development goals, with support to be established for specific tools and versions.

4 · Measure · In development, with first clients

Alacrity Insights

Insights combines production data from Pure with decision data from Player. Every production and every experience gets its own analytics free. The price rises only as Insights connects data across your viewers, across your productions, and finally across the industry.

Free · your production

Schedule against actual, pages and setups per day, budget burn and coverage. Plus views, completion and choice counts for every scene.

Pro · your viewers

Individual viewer journeys: the path each person took, time in each room or scene, replays, lead scores, trainee decision records and sponsor moments.

Benchmarks · the industry

How your production compares with anonymized, comparable productions. Contributing data is always opt-in, anonymized and free of content: no scripts and no footage.
