How it works

Made by us. Kept live by a yearly plan.

Every business experience is two things. First, we make it from start to finish. Then a yearly plan keeps it running and shows you what every viewer chose. Our crew plans every job on our own software, so each shoot is faster than the last.

Part 1 · One-time

Turnkey production

We handle all of it: scoping, story and branch design, capture (VR camera, drone, LiDAR), post and spatial audio. Then we compile it for Alacrity Player. You hand us a space or a problem, and we hand back something that runs.

Part 2 · Yearly

Player & Insights plan

Hosting and streaming to headsets, the web and phones. Device management for your Quest units. A set number of content updates each year (a new listing, a new hazard, a new season). And Insights: which rooms people visited, which call each trainee made, and what they came back to.

  1. Call20 minutes, with a demo on a headset or your phone.
  2. ScopeA fixed-scope written estimate. Nothing is booked until you approve it.
  3. PlanBranch map, schedule and call sheet in Pure Alacrity. You get an approval link.
  4. CaptureOur crew and our own Titan 11K and Canon R5 VR rigs. No rental delays.
  5. DeliverOn Alacrity Player, on any screen. Your Insights dashboard goes live.

Do my customers need a VR headset?

Not for long. Experiences play on Meta Quest today, and browser and phone playback is rolling out in early 2027, so anyone with a link can look around. The headset stays the premium version, for showings, classrooms and kiosks.

Why a yearly plan?

Because the value keeps coming. The plan covers hosting, updates and Insights: which rooms a buyer cared about, which call a trainee got wrong. You see that every week, not once.

Can we start small?

Yes. A single listing or a single scenario is a normal first project. We scope it in writing first, so you know exactly what you get before anything is booked.

Do you still make regular video?

Yes. Shoots, edits, colour and social content, from the same crew. Get an estimate.

Is Pure Alacrity really free?

Every software feature is free for every filmmaker, with no account needed. It has run real productions since March 2026. Paid storage, services and support are coming in 2027. Open it.
