Layer 1 · The Experience Machine

Real places: measured, filmed, changeable.

AI labs generate places that don’t exist. Headset scans capture rooms with no people in them. We capture real places, measured and filmed, and keep AI clearly labeled on top. One capture day, four layers on one coordinate map.

1 · Measured

LiDAR

Drone and handheld scans by our licensed operator: true dimensions, floor plan and scale, so anything placed in the space fits.

In development

Shipped on Quest

AI generation

Restage, add or redesign inside the twin at true scale. Always labeled as imagined, never mixed into the real footage.

The reality switch

One control flips between the filmed truth and the AI-imagined version.

A buyer redesigns the kitchen, then flips back and sees the real one, filmed. A factory buyer drops their own machine into a supplier’s floor plan, then watches the real line running. We’ll show you what’s possible without ever lying about what’s there.

Impact · Wilmington & North Carolina

Film as a 9-to-5. Steady, weekday, local work for the crews who know how to capture reality.

Wilmington built a world-class crew base over 40 years, and it rises and falls with whichever production is in town. Experience Machine work happens every weekday: one-day captures scheduled in Pure, home for dinner. Students graduate with verified credits and a reason to stay.

Questions

Asked, and answered plainly.

Ask something else
What is D.C Alacrity?

A Wilmington, North Carolina company creating the Experience Machine: real places you can step inside, change with AI and still trust. Pure Alacrity, our production platform, is how we make it at scale, and our films are where both get proven first.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. The headset is the premium version. Alacrity Player ships on Meta Quest today; WebXR links and iOS and Android gyro apps are in development, so every experience can be watched with no headset at all.

Is Pure Alacrity really free?

Yes, every software feature. It is live at dcalacrity.com/pure, needs no account and works with no internet, and it has run real productions since March 2026. In 2027 we will charge for what goes beyond the software: big cloud storage, services and live support.

Where did Pure Alacrity come from?

Sidequest began with an Episode 1 beta shot in Raleigh in November–December 2024, while D.C Agwu was in community college. Production continued in Raleigh and Wilmington in 2025, followed by Wilmington pickups in January–October 2026. Pure development began in January 2026, after a majority of Episodes 1–3 had been shot. The original shoots did not use Pure; the production problems helped define it. Read the full chronology.

Can I use the Alacrity Player in my own project?

The Player reads the bundle a story graph compiles to, with the schema pinned on both sides. For the runtime, the bundle format or a pipeline of your own, write to us.

Is the Sidequest Episode 1 on YouTube the final episode?

No. It is the beta, from the November–December 2024 Raleigh production. Final Episode 1 has not been released. Season 1 Part 1 covers a prologue and Episodes 1–3, about 28 shooting days across 2024–2026, and is in post. See the release status.

What could come after Sidequest’s first three episodes?

The world was written to run for years: a prequel era, the FBI side of the case, and squad missions you can play on Alacrity Player. Those are creative directions, not announced releases. Explore the world.

Where can I watch Right Here Right Now!?

It’s free as a native app on the Meta Horizon Store (Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S) and on SideQuest: the first live-action interactive VR film in the entire Southeast USA, with 16 scene nodes, 22 paths and five endings.

What is Prize Pool VR?

A live-action VR180 thriller where you choose between Eli and Maya in a campus water-gun competition with a million-dollar prize. Explore loyalty, romance and betrayal across a branching story. In pre-production; qualified for a UNCW CHSSA Research Grant. Explore Prize Pool VR.

Do you take client work?

Yes. Interactive experiences for real estate, training and destinations, plus regular shoots, edits, colour and social content. Start a project and you get a written estimate within two business days.

What’s live today, and what’s in development?

Live: Pure Alacrity (free), Alacrity Player on Meta Quest, Right Here Right Now!, and Full-Stack shoots for real estate. In development: the Experience Machine app, Gaussian splat twins, AI restyling and the reality switch, WebXR and phone playback, Bridge, Insights, Pure channels, certified crews, Alacrity Pass and Prize Pool VR. Follow any of them.
