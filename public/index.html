The Alacrity engine

One engine, from idea to audience.

In live action, every choice a viewer can make is another day of filming. That is why almost nobody makes interactive video. We built the software that plans the shoot, the player that runs the result, and the analytics that show what people chose.

1 · Make

Pure Alacrity

Write the story graph, then cost and schedule every branch and run the set. The graph and the stripboard are the same object.

Live · free 2 · Connect

Alacrity Bridge

Shot plans and the day’s records go into Premiere, Resolve and Unity as a real timeline, through OpenTimelineIO.

In development 3 · Deliver

Alacrity Player

Plays the finished experience on a Quest headset today, with browser and phone playback coming next.

Shipped 4 · Measure

Alacrity Insights

Production data from Pure plus every viewer decision from Player: who chose what, where they lingered, and what they replayed.

The capability

A written story becomes something people can play.

Game engines have done the second half for years. None of them know what a call sheet is — in live action a branch is a shooting day, not a line of script.

How it all fits together

Five stages1/5

1 · Author

The story graph

Scenes, choices and endings, written as a graph you can read. The interactive format carries pathway and choice lines beside the standard set, so the same file is a screenplay a person reads and a map the engine walks.

2 · Produce

Breakdown & schedule

Every branch costed and scheduled like any film. In live action a choice the audience makes is a scene the crew has to shoot both sides of, so the graph and the stripboard are the same object.

3 · Compile

One bundle

A single file. Schema 2.0.0, pinned on both sides and round-trip tested, so either half can change without the other silently breaking.

4 · Run

Alacrity Player

Unity reads the bundle and plays it. It is the runtime inside Right Here Right Now!, and what Prize Pool VR is being built against.

5 · Deliver

Wherever people are

Headset, a flat path, the web — the same graph. Right Here Right Now! is free on Meta Horizon and SideQuest.

Questions

Asked, and answered plainly.

Ask something else
What is D.C Alacrity?

D.C Alacrity creates interactive experiences people can step inside, powered by our own engine that takes any production from idea to audience, on any screen. We make them for businesses (Spaces, Training, Destinations), make our own titles, and give the production software to every filmmaker free. Founded in Wilmington, North Carolina.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. The headset is the premium version. Browser and phone playback is rolling out in early 2027, so every experience can be watched with no headset at all.

Is Pure Alacrity really free?

Yes, every software feature. It is live at dcalacrity.com/pure, needs no account and works with no internet, and it has run real productions since March 2026. In 2027 we will charge for what goes beyond the software: big cloud storage, services and live support.

Where did Pure Alacrity come from?

Sidequest began with an Episode 1 beta shot in Raleigh in November–December 2024, while D.C Agwu was in community college. Production continued in Raleigh and Wilmington in 2025, followed by Wilmington pickups in January–October 2026. Pure development began in January 2026, after a majority of Episodes 1–3 had been shot. The original shoots did not use Pure; the production problems helped define it. Read the full chronology.

Can I use the Alacrity Player in my own project?

The Player reads the bundle a story graph compiles to, with the schema pinned on both sides. For the runtime, the bundle format or a pipeline of your own, write to us.

Is the Sidequest Episode 1 on YouTube the final episode?

No. It is the beta, from the November–December 2024 Raleigh production. Final Episode 1 has not been released. Season 1 Part 1 covers a prologue and Episodes 1–3, about 28 shooting days across 2024–2026, and is in post. See the release status.

What could come after Sidequest’s first three episodes?

The world was written to run for years: a prequel era, the FBI side of the case, and squad missions you can play on Alacrity Player. Those are creative directions, not announced releases. Explore the world.

Where can I watch Right Here Right Now!?

It is free on Meta Horizon and SideQuest — North Carolina’s first interactive live-action VR film, 16 scene nodes across 22 paths.

What is Prize Pool VR?

A live-action VR180 thriller where you choose between Eli and Maya in a campus water-gun competition with a million-dollar prize. Explore loyalty, romance and betrayal across a branching story. In pre-production; qualified for a UNCW CHSSA Research Grant. Explore Prize Pool VR.

Do you take client work?

Yes. Interactive experiences for real estate, training and destinations, plus regular shoots, edits, colour and social content. Start a project and you get a written estimate within two business days.

How do I reach you?

For a project, start here. For anything else, use the contact page or write to support@dcalacrity.com.
