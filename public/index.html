The capability

A written story becomes something people can play.

Game engines have done the second half for years. None of them know what a call sheet is — in live action a branch is a shooting day, not a line of script.

How the three fit together

Five stages1/5

1 · Author

The story graph

Scenes, choices and endings, written as a graph you can read. The interactive format carries pathway and choice lines beside the standard set, so the same file is a screenplay a person reads and a map the engine walks.

2 · Produce

Breakdown & schedule

Every branch costed and scheduled like any film. In live action a choice the audience makes is a scene the crew has to shoot both sides of, so the graph and the stripboard are the same object.

3 · Compile

One bundle

A single file. Schema 2.0.0, pinned on both sides and round-trip tested, so either half can change without the other silently breaking.

4 · Run

Alacrity Player

Unity reads the bundle and plays it. It is the runtime inside Right Here Right Now!, and what Prize Pool VR is being built against.

5 · Deliver

Wherever people are

Headset, a flat path, the web — the same graph. Right Here Right Now! is free on Meta Horizon and SideQuest.

What is D.C Alacrity?

A technology and media company building the Experience Industry. It makes original properties, builds the software that makes them possible, and turns the way it works into systems other people can run. Founded in North Carolina.

Is Pure Alacrity really free?

Yes. It is live at dcalacrity.com/pure, needs no account and works with no internet. It has run real productions since March 2026.

Where did Pure Alacrity come from?

Out of Sidequest, not for it. The season and an internship at an established Wilmington production house made the broken workflow obvious, and development began in January 2026. The season itself was not made on it.

Can I use the Alacrity Player in my own project?

The Player reads the bundle a story graph compiles to, with the schema pinned on both sides. For the runtime, the bundle format or a pipeline of your own, write to us.

Where can I watch Right Here Right Now!?

It is free on Meta Horizon and SideQuest — North Carolina’s first interactive live-action VR film, 16 scene nodes across 22 paths.

What is Prize Pool VR?

The next interactive title: a branching live-action VR180 social thriller. 41 unbroken takes, nine endings, 5,535 routes and 12½–17½ minutes a viewing. The script, graph, board and budget are done; photography is next. Read about it.

Do you take client work?

Yes — shoots, edits, colour, social and destination films, at published rates for North Carolina businesses. We reply with a simple estimate within two business days.

How do I reach you?

One form, routed by topic, on the contact page — or write to support@dcalacrity.com directly.
